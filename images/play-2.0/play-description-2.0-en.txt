Should you grade that card? What is it really worth? Scan it and know in seconds.

Grade or Not is the collector's app for sports cards and trading card games. Scan a card to see its value raw and at every grade, get a clear grading verdict, check what to pay before you buy, and keep your whole collection in binders that track its value.

GRADE IT OR NOT?
Grade or Not compares what your card sells for raw with what it sells for graded. It takes out the grading fee, shipping both ways and the selling fees, then gives you a clear verdict:
• SEND IT: grading should leave you more money
• BORDERLINE: about the same, it depends on the grade it gets
• KEEP IT RAW: grading would likely cost more than it adds

HOW MUCH SHOULD I PAY?
Buying a raw card? Scan it and the app marks the flaws it can see in the photo (corners, edges, surface, centering) and shows an estimated value for that copy, so you can spot a good deal before you pay.

BINDERS THAT WORK FOR YOU
• Make your own binders, pick a cover, and swipe through them like the real thing. Your total estimated value is always on top.
• Binders that fill themselves: Worth grading, Graded cards, Raw cards, Losing value and more.
• Add what you paid and see how much each card is up or down.
• This week's moves: see which cards moved and which are losing value.
• Scan a card straight into a binder.

TRACK WHAT YOU SEND FOR GRADING
Pick the cards you send, add the cost and the date, and follow them until they come back. Then see whether their value went up after the cost.

EVERY PRICE IN ONE PLACE
• Sold prices raw and at every grade, from established price guides. When a card has no recorded sales yet, the app estimates from current listings and tells you so.
• Demand: how many sold in the last year and how many are listed right now.
• Already graded? Scan a slab to see what it is worth at its grade and what you keep after fees.
• Not sure it is the right card? Compare your photo with the matches and pick yours.
