## 5. New section: How long we keep information

We keep your account information and saved results until you delete them or your account. Full-size card photos are discarded after they are read. Usage records are deleted after 90 days. Cached price results expire within 24 hours. After you delete your account, remaining copies in backups are erased in the normal backup cycle, within about 30 days.

## 6. New section: Where your information is stored and how we protect it

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. We use encryption in transit, access controls that limit each account to its own data, and restricted administrative access. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under U.S. state privacy laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. You can access, correct or delete your information in the app, or by emailing support@gradeornot.net. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights; we will honor valid requests as required by law. The Service is not offered in the European Union.

## 8. New section: Do Not Track

We do not track you across third-party websites or apps, so we do not respond differently to browser Do Not Track signals.
